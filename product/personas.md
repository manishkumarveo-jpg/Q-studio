# Personas

+## Personas

| Persona | Objective | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | Securely operate the workspace | Needs one-time deployment bootstrap and account recovery procedure. |
| Admin | Configure people, metadata and access | Cannot inspect or reveal passwords. |
| Producer | Drive review and correction closure | Needs sortable scope, accurate ownership and trustworthy export. |
| Editor/Animator | Fix assigned work | Needs context, version fidelity and quick status/remarks. |
| Reviewer | Leave precise feedback | Needs simple player controls and annotation confidence. |
| Client/Approver | Review only a granted scope | Needs low-friction guest access with no workspace discovery. |
